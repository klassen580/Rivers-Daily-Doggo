📒 Transactions & Backup

Record transactions for the selected child. Download a backup to keep all three portfolios safe.

Deposits, withdrawals, dividends and fees adjust the app's manually tracked portfolio value. With holdings set up, deposits and dividends go to cash, and an ETF purchase or sale with units moves money between cash and that ETF (its price is updated from the trade). Without holdings, purchases and sales are recorded only. This is not a live brokerage feed. Check actual balances during the January review.

🔐 Backup & restore

Download an unencrypted JSON backup of all portfolios, transactions and annual-review dates. Keep it somewhere private. Restoring replaces the app's current data. Backups made with Beta 3.5 or newer also carry your parent PIN, so it works on a new device.
